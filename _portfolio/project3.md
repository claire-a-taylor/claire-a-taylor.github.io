  video: <iframe src="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1KnLst5tzzHRLG573C42mN8HRcvJVkLw7/preview" width="720" height="480" frameborder="0" scrolling="no" seamless="" allowfullscreen></iframe>
  text: The three activities depicted were created for an earlier release and destination before being edited and ported into other areas of the game. Unfortunately, they were built to handle a maximum of three players who had been matchmade together into a fireteam and were not set up to handle the drop-in/drop-out nature and large player counts of the public Open World areas. I needed to heavily alter them to function in a way that would accept other people joining or potentially leaving the area in the middle of them, while gracefully adding or cleaning themselves up for only those people. Not only did the logic behind adding the HUD indicators, buffs, debuffs, and progress timers/tracking need to be overhauled, but the VFX language needed to be changed to be more in line with the release themes. Once the events were finally set up to handle any number of people with only loose instance networking, I then needed to place them all by hand in 3-7 bubbles each across 7 destinations while respecting existing destination content, events, and design. This resulted in me curating over 115 different spaces with their own requirements and challenges for these three mini-content activities to occupy, as well as hooking them up to the open world schedulers to make them spawn and despawn over time, and only when the particular destination was the current focus of a larger rotating destination schedule.

entry2:
  subtitle: Public Event activity, curated and balanced per location and placed in the Open World
  video: <iframe src="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1nlXc7FBiJ79pnm7885CDOXrYxc-KJFld/preview" width="720" height="480" frameborder="0" scrolling="no" seamless="" allowfullscreen></iframe>
  text: The Flood Public Event was a 9-round delighter activity designed to spawn in after a normal Public Event had concluded as long as the destination was the current focus of a large rotating destination schedule. I needed to carefully choose Flood Event locations in each public bubble space across 7 destinations that already featured Public Events and hook them up to the public space scheduler as post-event spawns to existing Public Events of a high enough tier. In many spaces, I also had to be aware of any other events that could be existing in the same space and add a despawn rule to them so if the Flood Event activated nearby they would cull themselves and deactivate to not interfere. For each Flood Event location I had to carefully select spawn placements, squad composition, locations of spawned power-ups, and destructible target locations for rounds 3 and 7. Not only did I need to make sure encounters were balanced enough to provide a challenge for large groups of players, but I needed to also keep in mind the flow of battle, how players would respond to different squad compositions, and how best to escalate the intensity over time without feeling unfair or impossible. Besides placing the event in the open world I also had a large influence on the event itself by suggesting the destructible target rounds to break up the intense combat waves, a minimum time the overall event countdown would be reset to upon spawning the boss to give players who were down the wire a chance to finish strong and reward them, and in fine tuning the central VFX look and appearance timing to attract players to the area before and during the event.
